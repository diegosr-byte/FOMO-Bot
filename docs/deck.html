FOMO Bot

A personal agent that finds the tech events worth your time, says no to the rest, and helps you meet the right people.

Open source (MIT) · github.com/diegosr-byte/FOMO-Bot · Diego & Lukas

The problem, and who it helps

  • Three hackathons in three weeks. Each one meant hours reading the event page, finding out who the judges are, and checking every sponsor's docs.
  • Most events are found by luck, through a friend or a random invite.
  • Event lists match topics. They don't know your calendar, your history or your goal, so everything looks worth going to.
  • For: students, founders and builders in busy tech scenes like SF, who want the right rooms without the overload.

What it does

1. Learn you

Reads your Google Calendar and Gmail (Luma invites, past sign-ups) to infer interests and busy slots.

2. Find and verify

Searches for upcoming events and confirms each date and time on the event's own page. Unconfirmed events are dropped.

3. Rank by people

Scores events by who is there against your goal, and says no for you (anti-FOMO budget).

4. Act

Adds [Suggested] holds to Calendar and writes a cited Google Doc digest.

5. Prep and reach out

Prep card per person and an unsent Gmail draft. For hackathons: a judges, sponsors and past-winners brief.

6. Always on

Runs every Monday, and from your phone through Telegram.

How it works: no app code

The agent is Cline running one skill (.cline/skills/fomo-bot/). Install it with one script.

Cline

The runtime: the skill, cline schedule for Mondays, cline connect telegram for chat.

Parallel

Search and fetch to find and verify events. Deep research on judges, sponsors and people, with citations.

Composio

Picks the right tool and acts: Google Calendar, Gmail drafts and Google Docs.

Built with Claude Sonnet 5.5 through Cline. Every claim in the Doc carries a source link.

Safe by design, open by default

  • Creates Docs, Calendar holds and Gmail drafts only. It never sends, deletes or forwards email and never invites anyone.
  • Calendar holds use send_updates: none, no attendees and no Meet links.
  • Event pages and emails are treated as untrusted data. Telegram is locked to one user ID.
  • When something fails it says what failed and what it did instead. It never invents a date, venue or speaker.
  • Simulated in the demo: the busy blocks on the demo calendar (study group, family dinner, gym) were added by us. Everything else is a live run on real accounts.
