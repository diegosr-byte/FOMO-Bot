# Who FOMO Bot is for

Five people who'd use FOMO Bot every week. Each one has a different goal, and the goal changes who the bot looks for at an event. The personas are made up; the problems are real.

| | Persona | Goal | Who the bot finds for them |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lukas, visiting founder | Investors | Partners and angels in his space who are at events this week |
| 2 | Maya, solo technical founder | Cofounder | Builders with complementary skills, ex-founders |
| 3 | Daniel, B2B SaaS founder | Clients | Buyers and decision makers in his market |
| 4 | Priya, engineer looking for a job | Job in tech | Hiring managers and founders at companies hiring for her role |
| 5 | Sam, devrel at an AI startup | Something else: community and partners | Hosts, speakers and builders using similar tools |

---

## 1. Lukas: in SF for one week, looking for investors
- **Who:** a founder from Europe, in San Francisco for one week.
- **Building:** an AI agent for property managers (proptech).
- **Pain:** there are 40+ tech events this week. He can't go to all of them, and he has no idea which ones investors will actually attend.
- **What FOMO Bot does:** it picks 3 events where proptech-focused partners are speaking or judging. It builds his evening route between them, writes a prep card for each person, and gives contact cards with their X, LinkedIn and email.
- **Try it:** `find me events this week. goal: meet proptech investors`
- **The pitch line:** "I'm the real user. I'm a visitor in SF this week."

## 2. Maya: solo technical founder looking for a cofounder
- **Who:** an ML engineer who left Big Tech three months ago. She's building alone.
- **Building:** an evaluation tool for LLM apps.
- **Pain:** meetups are full of people pitching, not people looking to join something. She needs a go-to-market cofounder.
- **What FOMO Bot does:** it ranks hackathons and founder mixers by who's attending: ex-founders, product and GTM people, people who just left a startup. It skips events that are only investor pitches, and says why.
- **Try it:** `find me events this month. goal: find a business cofounder for my LLM eval tool`

## 3. Daniel: B2B SaaS founder looking for first clients
- **Who:** a founder with a working product and five pilot users.
- **Building:** compliance automation for fintech teams.
- **Pain:** most events he goes to are full of other founders, not buyers.
- **What FOMO Bot does:** it finds the events where heads of compliance and fintech CTOs speak or host. It drafts a short note to each one about the problem they mentioned in their talk, and lists their public contact routes.
- **Try it:** `find me events in the next 2 weeks. goal: meet heads of compliance at fintechs`

## 4. Priya: engineer looking for a job in tech
- **Who:** a backend engineer who recently moved to the Bay Area.
- **Building:** looking for a senior backend or infra role at an AI startup.
- **Pain:** online applications go nowhere, and a warm intro at an event is worth 100 applications. But which events have hiring managers?
- **What FOMO Bot does:** it picks events where founders and engineering leads from companies with open infra roles are speaking. It checks their careers pages. The outreach message asks for a short chat about the team and open roles.
- **Try it:** `find me events this week. goal: get a senior backend job at an AI startup`

## 5. Sam: developer advocate growing a community
- **Who:** a devrel at an AI startup, at 3-4 events every week.
- **Building:** a developer community around an open-source agent framework.
- **Pain:** too many events, too little time. Sam has to choose which to attend, which to sponsor, and who to invite as a speaker.
- **What FOMO Bot does:** it runs every Monday by itself. It ranks the week's events by which hosts and speakers use similar tools, and caps Sam at 3 events a week, with a reason for each skip.
- **Try it:** `find me events this week. goal: meet hosts and speakers in the AI agents community. limit: 3`

---

**What all five have in common:** too many events and too little time. The value is in the people at an event, not its topic. FOMO Bot reads their calendar and invites, ranks events by who is there for *their* goal, says no to the rest, and hands them one-tap ways to reach the right people. It never sends anything on its own.
